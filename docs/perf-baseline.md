`peak_rss_inherited_floor_bytes` is the driver's own RSS at fork: `ru_maxrss` is a high-water mark that starts from the parent's RSS, so a peak near the floor is inherited rather than earned. The driver generates the fixture in a separate process and keeps its footprint small; a Go or Python peak at or below that floor is not a real measurement of the implementation.

## Acceptance

- Go vs Python is a REPORTED result, not a gate. A Go loss on any metric is a recorded outcome and is shown above exactly as measured.
- The reader refreshes the `journal.sent_lines` gauge at most every 10 s, so whole-run wall time carries up to that much detection latency. Differences on the instrumented metrics (wall, entries/s, MB/s) within that bound, or with overlapping per-run min/max ranges, are reported as `no clear difference`.
- The `make bench-check` tripwire compares Go against THIS Go baseline only, on the primary metrics, and fails only on a >50% regression between compatible runs.
